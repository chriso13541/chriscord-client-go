Performance

MKVs, HEVC, 10-bit and other formats become MP4s on your computer before they're sent; videos that already play everywhere are sent as they are. Turn this off to send untranscoded video — anyone whose app can't play it gets Open in video player and Download instead.

Games, calls and everything else you're doing come first; transcoding uses whatever's left over (so it can take longer while you're busy).

Much faster and lighter on the CPU. Turn off to always use the CPU.

Screen Sharing

Sharing your screen

If your connection drops while you're sharing, the same screen or window is shared again as soon as you're back in the call, at the same settings. Off: the share ends when the connection drops.

Watching streams

Receive other people's streams at no more than the size and frame rate below, instead of whatever they're sharing at — less for this computer to download and decode. Their app sends a second, smaller version just for the people who ask for one.

Voice & Video Settings

100%

Brings your voice to a steady, normal loudness for everyone else, however quiet your microphone is. Turn off to send exactly what Input Volume gives.

100%

When someone's connection stutters, their voice can fall behind. This catches it back up by shortening the pauses between their words (and, if it has to, trimming a few milliseconds of speech without changing how they sound). Turn off to skip ahead instead.

Speak into your microphone and you should hear yourself out of your speaker, with your volume and sensitivity applied — exactly what others hear. The meter above moves while you're testing or in a call: the bar turns green when you're loud enough to be transmitted (and your speaking ring lights up).

Video

Camera preview

Only sizes and frame rates your camera actually supports are listed, and your camera is never asked for anything else. Turn your camera on during a call with the camera button next to mute.

Sounds

70%

One volume for all of the app's sound effects — joins and leaves, mutes and deafens, dropped calls and @mentions. It doesn't change how loud people sound in calls (that's Output Volume, above).
