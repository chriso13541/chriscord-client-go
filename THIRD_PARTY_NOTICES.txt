OpenMoji (emoji graphics)
-------------------------

- Source: https://github.com/hfg-gmuend/openmoji (release 17.0.0),
  color SVGs from openmoji-svg-color.zip and data/openmoji.json
- Credit: All emojis designed by OpenMoji – the open-source emoji and icon
  project (https://openmoji.org). This credit is also shown in the
  emoji picker's footer.
- License: Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International
  (CC BY-SA 4.0) — full text in frontend/licenses/openmoji-LICENSE.txt
- Included: the 1,914 standard emoji (frontend/emoji/svg/<hexcode>.svg),
  copied unmodified — no skin-tone variants and no OpenMoji "extras".
  frontend/emoji/emoji-data.js is generated from openmoji.json (hexcode,
  character, name, category, search tags).
- Also included: the OpenMoji color font (frontend/emoji/
  OpenMoji-color-glyf_colr_0.woff2, from openmoji-font.zip in the same
  release, unmodified), used so emoji typed in text boxes appear as
  OpenMoji rather than the operating system's emoji.
- ShareAlike applies to the emoji graphics themselves (and any modified
  versions of them), not to the rest of chriscord.
